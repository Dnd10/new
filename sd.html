Problem
Statement

You are given purchases and products tables. Each purchase records one paid order. Calculate total paid revenue for July 2025 and August 2025, then return only products where August revenue is at least 20% greater than July revenue.

  • Use only orders with status = 'paid'.
  • Round percentage lift to two decimals.
  • Sort by percentage lift descending, then product name ascending.
  • Products with no July revenue should be excluded.

WITH monthly AS (
  SELECT
    p.product_name,
    SUM(CASE
      WHEN pur.purchased_at >= DATE '2025-07-01'
       AND pur.purchased_at < DATE '2025-08-01'
      THEN pur.amount ELSE 0 END) AS july_revenue,
    SUM(CASE
      WHEN pur.purchased_at >= DATE '2025-08-01'
       AND pur.purchased_at < DATE '2025-09-01'
      THEN pur.amount ELSE 0 END) AS august_revenue
  FROM purchases pur
  JOIN products p
    ON pur.product_id = p.product_id
  WHERE pur.status = 'paid'
  GROUP BY p.product_name
)
SELECT
  product_name,
  july_revenue,
  august_revenue,
  ROUND(((august_revenue - july_revenue) / july_revenue) * 100, 2) AS pct_lift
FROM monthly
WHERE july_revenue > 0
  AND august_revenue >= july_revenue * 1.2
ORDER BY pct_lift DESC, product_name ASC;
